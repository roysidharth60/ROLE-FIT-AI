Add these TWO lines to backend/app/main.py (your existing file). Nothing else changes.

1) With the other imports at the top:
from app.applicant.router import router as applicant_router

2) Right after  app = FastAPI(...)  and the CORS middleware block:
app.include_router(applicant_router)

Install the two packages (inside your backend venv):
pip install python-docx python-multipart
